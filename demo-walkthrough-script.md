# Seen — 1-Minute Demo Walkthrough Script

*Timed for spoken delivery at a natural pace (~150 wpm). Bracketed cues tell you what to tap/show at that moment — practice once against a stopwatch and trim filler words if you're running long.*

---

**[0:00–0:08] — Permissions**
*(App launches, permission prompts appear)*

> "First launch, Seen asks for exactly three things — sleep and steps,
> calendar, and location for weather. Nothing more. This passive data never
> leaves the phone on its own — it only matters once I connect it myself."

**[0:08–0:20] — The scene**
*(Scene renders — bedroom/workspace illustration)*

> "Based on that data, the app builds a scene — not a mood scale, a room.
> Today it picked this one because my calendar's packed and I didn't sleep
> much. Every object in here — the laptop, the mug, the shoes — represents
> something that might have shaped my day."

**[0:20–0:35] — Tapping an item / reflection**
*(Tap the mug → question appears instantly → type a short answer → tap laptop too)*

> "I tap what actually stood out to me. The question here is specific to
> this object — pre-fetched the moment the scene loaded, so it's instant. I
> answer in my own words — nothing is inferred for me. I'll grab one more
> object..."

*(Tap "Save" / complete flow → reflection generates)*

> "...and once I'm done, Seen writes a short first-person reflection —
> built only from what I actually typed, never from raw sleep or calendar
> data I didn't connect myself."

**[0:35–0:45] — Edit**
*(Tap Edit, type a steering note: e.g. "make this more hopeful")*

> "If it doesn't sound right, I can steer it — tell it to sound more
> hopeful, more concise, whatever — and it rewrites the same facts in that
> tone. It never invents anything new, just re-tells it the way I asked."

**[0:45–1:00] — Patterns**
*(Navigate to Patterns tab)*

> "And this is Patterns — my week at a glance. A streak of how often I've
> reflected, a heatmap of sleep, movement, and calendar load, and two
> AI-surfaced insights: a pattern worth noticing, and what's actually been
> helping — both grounded in real days, not guessed."

---

## Delivery notes

- **Total spoken word count**: ~230 words — trim the bracketed narration lines (not the demo actions) if you're consistently running past 60 seconds.
- **Practice the taps against the words**, not the other way around — if the follow-up question doesn't appear instantly during a live run, keep talking through the pause rather than stopping to wait on it.
- **Have a backup profile ready** (Demo Profile switch in the Profile tab) in case the live device's real passive data doesn't produce an interesting scene on demo day.
- **If patterns data takes a moment to load** (the AI insight cards specifically), narrate the heatmap/streak first — those render immediately, non-AI — and let the insight cards fill in while you're still talking.
